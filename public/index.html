Using the marks

Short version: don't redraw them.

Please do

  • Hotlink these URLs directly; they are stable and versionless.
  • Use the SVG wherever the surface supports it; fall back to PNG only when it does not.
  • Keep clear space around the logo equal to the height of the icon's corner radius.
  • Place the logo on white, on #001F3F, or on a plain colour with sufficient contrast.

Please don't

  • Do not recolour, rotate, skew, outline or add effects to the marks.
  • Do not stretch; scale proportionally only.
  • Do not rebuild the wordmark in another typeface.
  • Do not place the logo on a busy photograph or a low-contrast background.
  • Do not use the marks to imply partnership or endorsement without written permission.
